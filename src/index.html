UNDER THE HOOD

Designed as a system.
Not just a chatbot.

MechRAG Lab combines a retrieval pipeline, document processing, governance, and evaluation. The goal is a foundation teams can examine and refine, rather than a black-box answer box.

Technical foundations include hybrid dense/lexical retrieval, reranking, workflow-based ingestion, source tracking, and role-aware controls. Some advanced capabilities remain under development or evaluation.

GOOD TO KNOW

Frequently asked
questions.

Clarity is part of the product. Here's what we can say about the platform today.

What document formats does it work with?

The ingestion foundation includes PDF, DOCX, spreadsheets and CSV, PPTX, text and Markdown, and images. Extraction quality varies and needs evaluation for each workflow.

How are answers connected to sources?

The system supports document citations, source-page information, and references to original material. Source-linked answers still require human verification.

How is document access controlled?

The codebase includes role-, department-, site-, and clearance-based access mechanisms, plus requests and audit support. Actual behavior depends on deployment configuration and validation.

Is the interface above a live AI demo?

No. The product preview uses synthetic sample data. This website does not connect to an AI model or process uploaded business documents.

Is the product available for a scoped pilot?

The project is oriented toward scoped demonstrations and pilot discussions. Specific access, acceptance criteria, and readiness need agreement and validation before any engagement.

Does it currently integrate Claude?

A native Claude or Anthropic integration has not been verified as implemented. Future integrations may be evaluated. This site does not claim a partnership or active Claude integration.
